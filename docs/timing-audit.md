Rule applied: `verdict.timeToProd.range` is the typical path for the path in `assumes`, based on the provider's official figures where they exist. Worst-case anecdotes (stalled reviews, forum reports) moved out of the range and into `assumes` as tail risk. Where no official timeline exists, the range is a conservative estimate and the evidence label is `inferred`. `hours` match the range (day=24, week=168, month=720).
